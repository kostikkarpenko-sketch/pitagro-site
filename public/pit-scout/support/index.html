PIT Scout Support

Get help with scouting visits, field boundaries, GPS, photo evidence and PDF exports.

Contact

Email pitagro.app@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Scout version and the steps that led to the problem. Remove private field details and client names from attachments you do not need to share.

GPS during a visit

Allow location while using the app and enable Precise Location for route recording. Start the visit while Scout is open. Pause or finish the visit when you are done. Check the saved route after the walk.

Print or share a report

Open a completed visit from Visit history. Choose Share Report to save the PDF to Files or send it to a destination you choose. Print / PDF opens the system print panel. On iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, a report that needs translation may prompt you to download an Apple language pack.

Keep a copy of your work

Scout saves visits and media on your device. Share important PDFs and export farm or field packages before clearing app data or reinstalling. See the PIT Scout Privacy Policy.
