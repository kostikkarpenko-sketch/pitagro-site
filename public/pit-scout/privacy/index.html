PIT Scout Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. This policy explains how PIT Scout for iPhone and iPad handles your field records and device permissions.

PIT Scout is provided by Kostiantyn Karpenko under the PIT Agro name. For privacy questions and requests, contact pitagro.app@gmail.com.

Field records and permissions

The app stores farm and field details, maps and zones you create, visits, GPS tracks, observations, photos, voice recordings, transcripts and report edits in its local workspace. The optional profile stores the name and organisation you choose to enter. You can use the main scouting tools without signing in.

When you grant location access, Scout uses it to show your position, attach locations to observations and record your route during an active visit. An active route can continue while the screen is locked. Pause or finish the visit to stop route recording. You can change location, camera, microphone, speech recognition, Bluetooth and local network access in Settings.

The camera and microphone capture evidence you request. The photo picker imports media you select. Locally supported speech recognition, Count, Measure and English PDF translation process the content on the device. iOS may ask for a language download. Optional compatible glasses use Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi to transfer media to Scout.

Maps and external providers

Apple Maps supplies the native basemap. Esri imagery can be requested for PDF maps and boundary suggestions. Map requests can reveal the area requested and connection information to the provider. Internet map layers can include OpenStreetMap and, when requested, satellite imagery queries to Element 84 Earth Search. The offline PDF can use saved geometry and the recorded route when imagery is unavailable.

See Apple Maps and Privacy, Esri's privacy statement, OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy and Element 84's privacy information.

Reports and field exchange

Scout prepares the English PDF on your device. Print and Share use the system services and destination you choose. Farm or field packages are files you control. A package can include boundaries, zones and selected soil measurements. PIT Agro does not run cloud synchronisation for the Scout workspace. Files you save or send can be retained by the destination you chose.

Optional cloud Summary in eligible pilot builds

If your build offers cloud Summary, it asks for consent before sending selected visit details, notes or transcripts, observation locations and up to ten photos to the Scout service hosted on ChatGPT Sites and to OpenAI for drafting. Sign-in restricts pilot access. The service uses date and usage counts tied to a hashed account identifier to enforce limits. The AI provider may retain request data for abuse monitoring under its applicable API data controls. Review the resulting draft before using it.

PDF translation, field exchange and recording audio do not invoke cloud Summary. See OpenAI API data controls and OpenAI's privacy policy.

Control, retention and support

Local records stay on your device until you delete them or clear the app's data. Device backups and files you export can keep separate copies. Before uninstalling, save any records you need. If you contact support, we use the details you send to respond and investigate the issue. We retain that correspondence as needed to resolve the request or meet applicable obligations.

Contact us to ask about, correct or request deletion of information we hold about you. Some providers may process information outside your country. Their linked policies describe their processing. Scout does not use your data for advertising tracking.
