PIT Sport Privacy Policy

Updated 9 November 2026

Who provides PIT Sport

PIT Sport is provided by Kostiantyn Karpenko as an individual under the PIT Agro name. Contact pitagro.app@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. This notice covers PIT Sport. Other PIT Agro applications and websites have their own notices.

Your training information

The app stores your optional nickname, goals, experience level, display preferences, exercise plans, workout dates, weights, repetitions, durations, notes and Growing progress. Training records can reveal information about your physical activity. Enter only information you want to keep in the app.

Local storage

The iPhone app keeps training records in its local app database. The website stores them separately in the browser at that website address. The app has no mandatory account or automatic upload of workout records to PIT Agro. Records do not automatically synchronise between devices or between the website and the iPhone app.

Records remain in the installation until you remove them or device, browser or backup actions remove them. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage can remove local records. Operating-system backups may retain copies according to your settings. Keep and check a separate export if you need a backup.

Exports, sharing and external links

When you export a backup or share a result, you choose a destination using your device. Exported files may include your training records and notes. The destination app or storage service processes the copy under its own privacy terms. Exports are not encrypted by PIT Sport.

Exercise and occupational-safety references open third-party websites. Those services receive connection information such as your IP address when you open them. No advertising, tracking SDK, HealthKit connection or automatic workout analytics is configured in the current app. Apple may process App Store transactions and diagnostics under its own policies.

Support correspondence

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and resolve related issues. Our basis for ordinary support correspondence is our legitimate interest in supporting the app and responding to enquiries.

The support mailbox uses Gmail. Google processes email under its own terms and privacy information, which may involve international processing. Our support-retention policy is 12 months after a case closes, subject to a necessary legal or dispute-related exception. This is not an automatic deletion guarantee. Avoid sending passwords, payment details or unnecessary health information.

Security and your choices

Protect your device and backups with a passcode and current software. The app does not configure its own database encryption or biometric lock. No system can guarantee absolute security.

You can edit your profile and plans, review history and export a backup. Device or browser controls can remove the installation's local data. Delete exported copies separately where you no longer need them. Because local training records are not automatically held by PIT Agro, we may be unable to recover them for you.

Privacy requests

Contact pitagro.app@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information actually held by the provider, or to object to its use. Rights depend on the circumstances and applicable law. You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or your relevant supervisory authority.

Audience and changes

PIT Sport's starter routines are intended for adults and provide general exercise guidance. They are not children's training programmes. Stop an exercise if you feel unwell or experience pain or dizziness and seek appropriate advice when unsure.

We will update this notice when the app or its data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
