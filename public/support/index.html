PIT Sample Support

PIT Sample is a practical field-sampling tool for planning routes, navigating between sampling points and recording sampling work.

Contact Support

For help with a problem, feedback or a feature request, email PIT Agro:

pitagro.app@gmail.com

Please include your device model, iOS version, app version and a short description of the issue.

If possible, describe what you were doing, what happened and what you expected to happen.

Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information. Remove private or client information from screenshots and share only what is needed to explain the issue.

Common Questions

Why does PIT Sample use my location?

Location supports your position on the field map, navigation between sampling points, GPS tracks and sampling progress. If your position is unavailable, check that location access is enabled for PIT Sample in your device settings.

Where are my sampling records stored?

PIT Sample v1.0 stores field and sampling records locally on your device. Keep exported copies of important records somewhere you control. Before removing the app or its local data, make sure you have saved the records you need.

How do I report an issue or request a feature?

Email pitagro.app@gmail.com with the details above. For a feature request, describe the fieldwork task and how the proposed change would help.

Where can I read about privacy?

Read the PIT Sample Privacy Policy for information about location, sampling records, exports and support communications.
