PIT Sample v1.0

PIT Sample Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies to the PIT Sample mobile application and related PIT Agro support services. It explains how information is used in the current PIT Sample v1.0 field-sampling workflow.

2. Location Data

PIT Sample uses device location, including precise GPS location, for its core field-sampling functionality. This includes:

  • displaying your position in a field;
  • navigating between sampling points;
  • recording the route or GPS track followed during sampling;
  • calculating distance and sampling progress.

Location is used to provide the app's field functionality. It is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. Field and Sampling Records

PIT Sample may process information you create or enter, including:

  • field boundaries;
  • sampling routes and sampling points;
  • sample identifiers and timestamps;
  • completed sampling records and GPS tracks;
  • your sampler or operator name, if you choose to enter it.

PIT Sample v1.0 stores field and sampling records locally on your device. This policy does not describe a PIT Agro cloud-storage or cloud-synchronisation service for those records.

4. Exports and Sharing

You may choose to export or share sampling records, reports, labels or geospatial files. You decide where to save them and with whom to share them.

When you share data with another application, cloud service, laboratory, client or other recipient, that recipient's privacy practices may apply. Exported copies are separate from the records stored within PIT Sample.

5. Maps and Third-Party Services

PIT Sample may display third-party map tiles, satellite imagery or geographic content. To deliver these resources, the providers may receive standard technical information, such as your IP address and the map resources requested by the app.

The providers' own privacy practices apply to their processing of that information.

6. Support Communications

If you contact PIT Agro for support, feedback or feature requests, information you voluntarily supply may be used to investigate and respond to your request. Please share only the information needed to explain the issue.

7. Advertising and Tracking

  • PIT Sample does not use third-party advertising.
  • PIT Sample does not track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes.
  • PIT Agro does not sell users' personal data.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored PIT Sample records remain under your control on your device. Removing local app data does not remove copies you have exported or shared elsewhere.

Exported files may remain wherever you have saved or shared them, including other applications, device backups, cloud storage or a recipient's systems. Those copies need to be managed separately using the relevant service or by contacting the recipient.

9. Children

PIT Sample is a professional agricultural field tool and is not directed to children.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated as PIT Sample develops, including if services such as accounts, cloud synchronisation, analytics or integrations are introduced. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit PIT Agro Support.
