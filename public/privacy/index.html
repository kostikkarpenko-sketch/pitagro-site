PIT Sample Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

1. Scope

PIT Sample is a field-sampling app provided by Kostiantyn Karpenko, an individual operating under the PIT Agro name. This notice covers PIT Sample and related PIT Agro support. For privacy questions, contact pitagro.app@gmail.com or visit PIT Agro Support.

2. Location Data

PIT Sample uses device location, including precise GPS location, for its core field-sampling functionality. This includes:

  • displaying your position in a field;
  • navigating between sampling points;
  • recording the route or GPS track followed during sampling;
  • calculating distance and sampling progress.

Location is used to provide the app's field functionality. You can change location permission in your device settings. Some features will not work without it, and changing permission does not erase locations already recorded. GPS accuracy varies.

The app may request camera permission when you choose to take a photograph or use an image feature, and Bluetooth permission when you choose to connect compatible equipment such as PIT Point. You can change these permissions in your device settings.

3. Field and Sampling Records

PIT Sample may process information you create or enter, including:

  • field boundaries;
  • sampling routes and sampling points;
  • sample identifiers and timestamps;
  • completed sampling records and GPS tracks;
  • farm and field names, areas, crops, work orders and details you enter;
  • your sampler or operator name, if entered, and a locally generated operator identifier used for work orders;
  • manually entered soil laboratory results and laboratory details.

These records are stored locally on your device; PIT Sample does not automatically synchronise them to a PIT Agro cloud account. Previous test versions may also have saved soil health records and photographs, which can remain on the device. Your operating system or a service you choose may make device backups. Deleting the app or its data can remove local records, so keep a separate backup and check what it includes.

4. Exports and Sharing

You may choose to export or share an order, sampling record, report, label, geospatial file or backup. These can contain field locations, operator details, records or photographs. You decide where to save them and with whom to share them.

When you share data with another application, cloud service, laboratory, client or other recipient, that recipient's privacy practices may apply. Exported copies are separate from the records stored within PIT Sample.

The app's own backup does not currently include work orders. A shared order file does not contain all sampling records or the device's operator identity. Check the backup screen and contents before relying on a copy to replace your original records.

5. Maps and Third-Party Services

On iPhone, PIT Sample uses Apple Maps to display the map. Some versions or optional features also request map imagery from Esri and its data suppliers, for example for automatic boundary detection or a PIT Point map view. Requests can reveal the area requested, IP address and other connection information to the relevant provider. See Apple Maps and Privacy and Esri's privacy statement.

If you connect optional PIT Point equipment, the app sends the information needed for the selected function over Bluetooth. Depending on the mode, this can include navigation information, a planned route, field boundary or map imagery. A recorded GPS track is not included in the prepared map image.

Some earlier test versions offered NDVI. When requested, that feature could send a field boundary and date range to Element 84 Earth Search to find imagery. See Element 84's privacy information.

6. Support Communications

If you contact PIT Agro, we use your contact details, message and any attachments you provide to respond and resolve the enquiry. Please share only the information needed to explain the issue.

Our retention policy is to review closed support correspondence and attachments for deletion 12 months after the enquiry is closed. We may retain a particular item longer where necessary for a legal obligation, dispute or another justified reason, and review that reason. Support email is handled using Google Gmail. Messages moved to Gmail Trash normally remain there for 30 days before deletion; other backups or copies may follow separate deletion cycles. See Google's deletion information.

7. Subscriptions and Payments

If subscriptions are available and you subscribe, Apple handles payment and subscription management through your Apple Account. PIT Sample uses information from Apple's purchase services to determine access; PIT Agro does not receive your payment-card details through the app. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

8. How and Why Information Is Used

Information is used to provide requested sampling features, maintain records and settings on the device, support optional equipment, answer enquiries, maintain security and meet applicable obligations. Much of the information you enter is processed on your device and is not sent to PIT Agro.

When we handle a support enquiry, we rely on our legitimate interests in answering the request, resolving problems and keeping a reasonable record of the outcome. Where processing is necessary to perform a contract with you, we rely on that contract; where we must retain or disclose information to meet a legal obligation, we rely on that obligation. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests as explained below.

Our service providers can process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Google describes its international transfer arrangements, including the UK extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and contractual safeguards where applicable, in its data transfer information. Apple, Esri and Element 84 describe their own processing and transfer practices in their linked privacy information. Contact us for details of safeguards relevant to information we hold about you.

9. Advertising and Tracking

  • PIT Sample does not use third-party advertising.
  • PIT Sample does not track users across apps or websites for advertising purposes.
  • PIT Agro does not sell users' personal data.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored records remain on your device until you delete them using available app or device controls or remove the app, subject to device backups. Removing local app data does not remove copies you have exported or shared elsewhere.

Exported files may remain wherever you have saved or shared them, including other applications, device backups, cloud storage or a recipient's systems. Those copies need to be managed separately using the relevant service or by contacting the recipient.

To ask about personal information held by PIT Agro, contact pitagro.app@gmail.com. Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your information, restrict or object to its use, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent where we rely on it. Legal exceptions can apply. If you are in the UK, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

11. Children

PIT Sample is a professional agricultural field tool and is not directed to children.

12. Changes

This policy may be updated as PIT Sample develops, including if services such as accounts, cloud synchronisation, analytics or integrations are introduced. Any updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or support, visit PIT Agro Support.
